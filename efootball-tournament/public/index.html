How the tournament works

Every stage narrows the field. Only the strongest players move on.

1

Group stage

1,000 players are split into 10 groups of 100. Each group has 10 pools of 10 playing round robin.

2

Second pooling

The top 10 from each group (100 players in total) form a new group, split into 10 pools of 10. The top 2 in each pool advance.

3

Final pools

The top 20 are split into 4 groups of 5. The top 2 in each group reach the quarter-finals.

4

Knockout

Quarter-finals, semi-finals and the final. One loss and you are out.
